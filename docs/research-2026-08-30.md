# HUNTIQ public retailer research — 2026-08-30

## Home Depot

Current Home Depot public guidance supports HUNTIQ's store-specific verification model:

- Home Depot's price-check guidance says shoppers should select a local store to see the correct local price and availability.
- Home Depot states online and in-store pricing can intentionally differ because of local pricing, promotions, and inventory conditions.
- Home Depot site footers continue to state that local store prices may vary and that displayed availability does not guarantee inventory.
- Home Depot's current price-match policy excludes clearance and closeout items. HUNTIQ must therefore treat a clearance observation as store-specific evidence, not as a portable price that another store is expected to honor.

Implementation consequence: continue storing `retailer + sku + storeId + observedAt + price + inventory + provenance`, require fresh re-verification before high-priority alerts, and never imply inventory certainty.

Sources checked 2026-08-30:
- https://www.homedepot.com/c/price-match-and-price-check
- https://www.homedepot.com/c/view-directory

## Costco

Costco Same-Day publicly states that its delivery prices are marked up above local warehouse prices. That means Costco.com/Same-Day observations should not be treated as direct warehouse-price proxies in HUNTIQ.

Costco customer service also states, in a document published 2026-03-03, that Costco does not price match other retailers.

Implementation consequence: if Costco is added later, HUNTIQ should distinguish warehouse, Costco.com, and Same-Day/Instacart observations as separate channels and avoid comparing them as if they were the same shelf price.

Sources checked 2026-08-30:
- https://sameday.costco.com/store/costco/pages/pricing-policy
- https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1162/~/does-costco-price-match-with-other-retailers%3F

## Tractor Supply

Tractor Supply's Terms of Use were last updated February 19, 2026 and explicitly say that Site prices may vary across geographic markets and between the website and physical stores. Its current price-match policy also excludes clearance items, close-outs/liquidations and advertising errors.

Tractor Supply's Neighbor's Club terms add an important HUNTIQ restriction: resellers are prohibited from extracting, scraping, mining, copying or otherwise collecting Site information in connection with downstream resale activity. HUNTIQ therefore must not build a resale-oriented Tractor Supply web scraper from those public pages.

Implementation consequence: classify Tractor Supply as `restricted research / authorized-data only` for automated collection. Public pages may inform source policy and channel semantics, but any production ingestion should use an expressly authorized feed/API/partnership. If authorized data becomes available later, preserve `storeId`/channel because online and store pricing can differ.

Sources checked 2026-08-30:
- https://www.tractorsupply.com/tsc/cms/policies-information/customer-solutions/terms-and-conditions-of-use
- https://www.tractorsupply.com/tsc/cms/neighbors-club-terms-and-conditions
- https://www.tractorsupply.com/tsc/cms/price-match
