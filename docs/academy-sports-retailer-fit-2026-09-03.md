# Academy Sports + Outdoors retailer fit — 2026-09-03

Public policy review only; no account access used.

## HUNTIQ implications
- Academy states that online and retail-store prices may differ and that it may maintain different prices by sales channel. HUNTIQ must keep store-local and online price history isolated.
- Academy may refuse or cancel orders when an item is listed at an incorrect price. Extreme anomalies therefore require repeat confirmation and must not be represented as guaranteed transactions.
- Competitor price matching is a checkout rule: qualifying identical/available items can be matched and Academy says it may beat a verified competitor price by 5%, subject to quantity and policy limits. Approved matches belong in acquisition economics, not raw shelf-price history.
- Purchase price adjustments may apply within 14 days, while clearance and timed-event purchases are excluded. Post-purchase adjustments remain contingent value until approved.
- Store pickup is location-specific; items are not charged until pickup, and customers are notified when an order is actually ready. Product-page pickup eligibility is useful availability evidence but is weaker than a ready-for-pickup confirmation.
- Current offer terms say selection, prices, offers and exclusions can vary online and by store, quantities may be limited, no rain checks may apply, and discounts often cannot be combined with price matches. Promotion stacking must therefore fail closed unless eligibility is confirmed.
