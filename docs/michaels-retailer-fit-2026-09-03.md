# Michaels retailer fit — 2026-09-03

Public policy review only; no account access used.

## HUNTIQ implications
- In-store purchases may be matched to an identical item on Michaels.com, subject to stock, identity and approval restrictions. Treat an approved match as checkout/acquisition economics, never raw shelf-price history.
- Online price adjustments can apply within seven days for qualifying identical items. Post-purchase adjustments are not guaranteed acquisition-price evidence before approval.
- Clearance, closeout, liquidation, pricing/typographical errors, limited-quantity offers and third-party marketplace listings are excluded from the online price guarantee. HUNTIQ must never imply a suspected pricing error is guaranteed to be honored.
- Coupons have item/category exclusions and generally cannot be stacked on the same item. Coupon-adjusted cost stays separate from raw shelf history.
- Michaels Rewards has no cash value. Model earned rewards as deferred retailer value until actually redeemed, not as cash-price reduction.
- Store pickup eligibility depends on selected-store inventory and assortment; preserve store identity and timestamp availability.
