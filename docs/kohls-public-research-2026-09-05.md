# Kohl's public retailer research — 2026-09-05

## HUNTIQ modeling rules

- Keep Kohl's price history scoped by location and channel. Kohl's states that pricing, promotions, merchandise availability, and Kohls.com versus store pricing can vary by location/channel.
- Do not use Kohl's Regular or Original price as evidence of actual market value. Kohl's states those values may be former or future offered prices for the item or a comparable item, actual sales may not have occurred at those prices, and intermediate markdowns may have occurred.
- Treat a price match as conditional acquisition economics, not historical shelf-price evidence. Kohl's stores may match Kohls.com or qualifying local competitor in-store prices under stated conditions, while Kohls.com has its own direct-competitor matching rules.
- Treat the two-week price-adjustment window as conditional post-purchase economics. Clearance and BOGO purchases are excluded, and coupons must still be valid to carry into an adjustment.
- Treat pickup availability as availability evidence until the ready-for-pickup confirmation arrives. Kohl's says eligible pickup orders are generally processed within two hours and customers receive an email when the order is ready.
- Keep Kohl's Cash and Kohl's Rewards separate from cash paid at checkout. Rewards convert later into Kohl's Cash, and pickup incentives are issued after a qualifying pickup; they must not automatically inflate immediate reseller profit or ROI.

## Product implication

For HUNTIQ, the strongest anomaly baseline is the item's verified local/channel observations and real selling market evidence. Kohl's own Original/Regular anchor must never substitute for actual selling price, which aligns with HUNTIQ's core rule to value a deal by real market value and expected net profit rather than MSRP-like anchors.
