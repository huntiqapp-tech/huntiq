# Target public retailer research — 2026-09

Public-source findings for HUNTIQ ingestion/economics modeling:

- Target states that pricing, promotions, styles, and availability may vary by store and online. Treat store and Target.com observations as distinct channels.
- Online clearance and in-store clearance are explicitly separate; Target says in-store clearance pricing is not visible online. A missing online clearance price is therefore not evidence that the store lacks a lower clearance price.
- In-store price matches from a different Target store are excluded, reinforcing store-local price identity.
- Target reserves the right to deny purchases and limit quantities per guest; quantity availability must not be interpreted as guaranteed purchasable quantity.
- Price matching excludes clearance/closeout/liquidation, typographical-error pricing, gift-card promotions, bundles, rebates, and several coupon combinations. HUNTIQ should not model a later price match as guaranteed recovery on these deal classes.
- Target Circle deals require account identification for proof. Circle Bonuses and other personalized/account-dependent discounts must remain qualified customer economics, not anonymous raw shelf price.
- Target says some prices may differ between locations due to market conditions and personalized Target Circle savings may use limited information such as location and shopping history. Preserve observation context and avoid collapsing personalized prices into a universal baseline.
