# Sam's Club retailer fit — 2026-09-05

Public-source research only. No member account or authenticated Sam's Club session was used.

## HUNTIQ rules

- Preserve club/location and channel identity. Sam's Club says prices, unit prices and available quantities may vary by Club and date, while its Instant Savings pages also state that prices may vary in club and online. A Sam's Club observation must therefore not become a single national price baseline.
- Treat Instant Savings as conditional member acquisition economics, not raw shelf-price history. Current Sam's Club materials say Instant Savings are membership-based, availability/date limited, and may carry item limits. Curbside savings depend on the offers available to the paying member on the payment date.
- Keep Sam's Cash separate from immediate purchase price. Plus membership currently advertises 2% Sam's Cash back, while promotional Sam's Cash may post later and can be reversed when a qualifying purchase is returned. HUNTIQ should not subtract future Sam's Cash from the observed price unless the reward is actually earned and usable by that buyer.
- Treat Curbside Pickup readiness as unconfirmed until fulfillment advances. Sam's Club terms say selected pickup times are targets, not guarantees, and merchandise is subject to availability. A displayed pickup slot or stock signal is therefore availability evidence, not secured inventory.
- Preserve weighted-item uncertainty. For fresh weighted merchandise, the final charge depends on the applicable unit price and actual purchased weight; the online value can be an authorization ceiling rather than the final acquisition amount.

## Product implications

For Sam's Club opportunities HUNTIQ should retain club/location, fulfillment channel, observed member price, Instant Savings eligibility and limit, weighted-item status, and deferred Sam's Cash separately. Price-history and anomaly evidence should use the observed price in its exact club/channel context. Profit and ROI should only incorporate member-specific discounts or rewards when eligibility and realization are known.
