# Ulta Beauty — public retailer modeling notes

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

## HUNTIQ disposition

**Restricted research only. Do not enable Ulta Beauty as a commercial scraping/data-collection or reseller-acquisition source without express written approval from Ulta Beauty.**

## Modeling and compliance rules

- Ulta's current Terms grant only personal, noncommercial Site use and expressly exclude resale/commercial use, collection/use of product listings, descriptions or prices, and data-mining/robot extraction without express written consent. HUNTIQ must therefore not scrape or persist Ulta product-price data from the public Site for commercial operation unless Ulta gives written permission.
- Ulta states that product sales are for personal use and may not be connected with resale/commercial use without express prior written approval. Ulta may limit or prohibit sales to dealers/resellers. HUNTIQ must not present Ulta as an approved retail-arbitrage acquisition source unless that approval is obtained and recorded.
- Keep ulta.com, store, mobile-app and third-party marketplace observations isolated. Ulta says online pricing can differ from stores or mobile applications, and pricing/availability remain subject to checkout confirmation.
- A displayed or temporarily mispriced item is not guaranteed acquisition evidence. Ulta can correct a price, refuse an order or cancel it. HUNTIQ should not treat an unconfirmed web price as possessed inventory or guaranteed profit.
- Coupons and rewards belong only in transaction-specific acquisition economics after eligibility is confirmed. They never become raw shelf-price history or resale market value.
- Ulta Beauty Rewards points can reduce qualifying purchases, but redemption excludes categories such as shipping, taxes and UB Marketplace products. Points therefore remain account-specific acquisition value, not a universal price observation.
- Replenish & Save currently advertises 5% off eligible recurring orders plus free shipping, but it is subscription-specific, requires program terms at enrollment, and benefits can change. It must not establish the anonymous public-price baseline.
- Pickup and same-day delivery remain fulfillment evidence, not possession. Store availability and delivery eligibility vary, and the customer receives readiness/fulfillment confirmation after ordering.
