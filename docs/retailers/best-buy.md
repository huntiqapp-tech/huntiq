# Best Buy — public modeling notes

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

## HUNTIQ rules

- Keep Best Buy Marketplace seller observations separate from Best Buy-sold observations. Marketplace products are excluded from Best Buy's Price Match Guarantee and should not establish the same retailer baseline.
- Keep clearance, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned condition histories isolated from new public-price history. Best Buy explicitly excludes those categories from price matching.
- Treat a successful price match as transaction-specific acquisition economics, not as evidence that the ordinary public shelf price changed. Best Buy limits matching to current, identical, immediately available new products and excludes many promotion types.
- Keep member-only pricing/rewards out of anonymous public history. My Best Buy Plus/Total members earn points on qualifying purchases and can receive exclusive member pricing; those are customer-specific economics.
- Treat newly earned points as deferred value. As of 2026-06-04, Plus and Total members earn 1% back on eligible purchases, with higher rewards possible when qualifying Best Buy Credit Card rewards are stacked.
- Require fulfillment identity on observations. Store pickup, shipping, alternate pickup and same-day delivery are distinct fulfillment states; an order should not be treated as possessed until the applicable ready/fulfillment confirmation exists.
- Outlet/clearance/open-box observations remain separate acquisition channels because pricing, warranty and price-match treatment differ from ordinary new merchandise.
