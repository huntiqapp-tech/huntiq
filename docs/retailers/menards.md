# Menards — public modeling notes

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

## HUNTIQ rules

- Keep the advertised purchase price separate from Menards' "Value After Rebate." Menards states the rebate is not an instant reduction at the original purchase; it is a mailed Rebate Credit Check for future in-store merchandise purchases.
- Treat a newly earned rebate as deferred value, not as a lower observed shelf price and not as guaranteed current-deal profit. It requires submission and later processing, and the resulting credit is for future in-store use rather than MENARDS.COM purchases.
- Preserve sale-window dates independently from rebate-window dates. Public product pages can show an Everyday Low Price, a sale price with its own expiration, and an 11% rebate with a different expiration.
- Preserve fulfillment identity. Public product pages can be store-only, pickup-capable, delivery-capable, or unavailable for online purchase; those states are not interchangeable price-history observations.
- Do not treat a displayed "Value After Rebate" as an acquisition-cost observation unless the model explicitly represents deferred rebate value and redemption friction separately.
- Exclude delivery charges, processing fees and other rebate-ineligible charges from rebate value calculations. Menards' public rebate terms expressly exclude those categories.
- Rebate-dependent opportunities should carry an execution caveat because the customer must submit documentation and wait for processing before receiving future purchasing power.
